Repiping in Saratoga Springs, UT

Repiping is a big job, so we start by checking whether you need one. Our licensed plumbers assess pinholes, discolored water and low pressure in Saratoga Springs homes.

Updated 2026-10-15 · Written by the Saratoga Springs Express Plumbing team

Call (385) 488-1892

Repiping in Saratoga Springs, UT means replacing the water supply pipes inside a house, either all of them or one section, with new copper or PEX. It is a major job, and often it is not needed. A single bad fitting is a repair. This page covers the symptoms that suggest a repipe, how our newer homes differ from older ones nearby and what the job looks like. Our 24/7 dispatch handles leaks as they happen.

Symptoms first: what the pipes are telling you

Signs that point toward a repipe

  • Pinhole leaks that appear in different places over a short time
  • Brown, orange or yellow water after the line has been idle
  • Low pressure at several fixtures but not all
  • Repeated repairs on the same line
  • Green stains or crust on copper fittings
  • Wet spots on walls or ceilings with no obvious source

One symptom is a clue. Several together are a pattern. If you only see one failure, ask for a repair quote first.

Why Saratoga Springs repipes are usually about a defect or a remodel

Saratoga Springs became a city in 2001. The population was about 1,003 in 2000 and 17,781 in 2010, so most homes here were built after 2000. That means full-house repipes from age are rare. More often the cause is a specific defect, a poor fitting, a freeze damaged line or a remodel that opens walls anyway.

Older homes in the nearby towns are where full repipes come up more. The Lehi historic core was settled in 1850, and American Fork has older neighborhoods as well. If you own an older house in Lehi or American Fork, ask for an inspection of the pipe material before you assume it needs replacing.

Galvanized and aging pipe

Galvanized steel pipe corrodes from the inside. Rust narrows the opening, pressure drops and water turns brown. Once the pipe is that far gone, repair patches fail in new places. In a house with galvanized supply, replacing it is usually the lasting fix.

We confirm the material by sight and with a magnet and scratch test at an exposed section. Do not assume from age alone. A sound copper line can last a long time, and we say so when it does.

Drywall access and how a repipe is staged

Repiping means opening walls. We try to keep holes small, use existing chases, cut at tees and follow plumbing walls. PEX with a manifold helps because each fixture gets its own line. Most openings are patched with drywall and left ready for paint.

The job runs in stages. We pace it so you can keep some water on.

A typical repipe, step by step

  1. Inspect and planWe find shutoffs, mark routes and agree on access cuts.
  2. ProtectFloors and furniture are covered and dust control is set.
  3. Rough inNew lines go in alongside the old and connect at the main.
  4. Pressure testWe test the new system before closing any walls.
  5. Cut over and patchThe old lines are disconnected and the access holes are patched.

Permit rules vary by job, so ask the Saratoga Springs building department before work starts. Call 811 at least three business days before any digging outside.

What affects the price of a repipe

Home size and fixtures

More bathrooms and fixtures mean more pipe and more connections.

Material

PEX and copper are priced differently.

Wall and ceiling access

Finished walls, slabs and tile cost more to open and close.

Partial or whole house

Replacing one run is a smaller job.

Drywall repair

Some jobs include patching, others hand the finish to a drywall contractor.

A note on insurance

Homeowners insurance usually does not pay to replace aging pipes, but may cover sudden water damage. Ask your insurer.
