Drain cleaning in Saratoga Springs, UT means clearing a blocked sink, tub, shower, floor drain or main line with a cable machine or hydro-jetting so wastewater flows to the city sewer again. If water is rising in a tub or a toilet gurgles whenever the washer drains, our 24/7 emergency line is open right now.

While you wait for the plumber, stop running water in the house, keep the washer and dishwasher off, and keep kids and pets away from standing water. Those three steps keep a backup from spreading. Same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows, and nights and weekends are answered the same way as weekday calls.

When a clogged drain is an emergency

One slow bathroom sink can wait until morning. The signs below cannot, because each one means wastewater has nowhere to go and will find the lowest opening in the house.

Call the 24/7 line right away if you see

  • Water or sewage coming up through a floor drain, tub or shower drain
  • More than one fixture backing up at the same time, such as a tub that gurgles when the toilet flushes
  • A toilet that rises toward the rim and will not settle after one plunge
  • A basement or garage floor drain that bubbles or smells of sewage
  • A kitchen sink that stays full after you have stopped the dishwasher

Do not pour a second bottle of chemical opener into a line that is already full. The liquid sits against the clog, and it can splash on a plumber's skin when the line is opened. Shut off the water you can control, move rugs and boxes off the floor, and call us.

What Saratoga Springs drains tell us about the clog

Most homes here were built after the city incorporated in 2001, so drain lines are usually plastic with smooth joints. That changes what we find. Clogs in newer homes tend to be soft and recent: hair and soap film in tubs, grease and food scraps in kitchen lines, and wipes labeled flushable that never break down.

The city gives a useful rule of thumb on its sewer page. Saratoga Springs Public Works says backups inside a building are generally caused by blockages in the building's own piping, and the city will inspect the sewer main in the street to verify. You can use that to narrow things down before anyone arrives.

A quick way to place the blockage

  • One fixture is slow or stopped: the clog is almost always in that fixture's own branch line
  • Two or three fixtures on the same floor are slow: the clog is likely in the branch they share
  • Every drain in the house is slow, or the lowest drain gurgles first: the blockage is in the main house drain or the line to the street

If the main line is the problem, we pull the cleanout cap, run a cable and look at the line with a camera. If the street main is full, we tell you to call Public Works about the city side. Neighborhoods such as Harvest Hills, Pelican Bay and The Benches all drain to the same city system, so the same test works in each of them.

How we find and clear the blockage

A drain call starts with questions and a look at the fixtures. We want to know which drains are affected, when it started and what went down the line that day. Then we pick the least invasive tool that will work.

  1. LocateWe test each fixture and find the nearest cleanout so the cable enters as close to the clog as possible.
  2. ClearA drum or sectional cable machine breaks through the blockage. The cutter head is matched to the pipe size, so a small kitchen line and a four-inch main get different heads.
  3. LookOn repeat clogs and main lines we run a drain camera to see what caused the stoppage, instead of assuming the cable fixed it.
  4. Prove itWe run water through the line for several minutes and watch the lowest drain. You see it flowing before we pack up.

Kitchen sink drain clogs

Kitchen lines clog more often than any other branch in the house. Cooking fat cools inside the pipe and lines the walls, and each rinse adds a little food debris until the opening is half its size. A garbage disposal pushes more scraps into that narrowed line.

We cable the branch first, then judge the pipe wall. If the cable comes back coated in grease, one pass is not enough and we recommend hydro-jetting to scour the pipe clean. If the disposal itself is jammed or leaking, our garbage disposal repair page covers that work.

Drain snaking and cabling: what it does and does not do

A cable punches a hole through a clog and pulls some of it out. It is the right tool for hair, paper and soft blockages, and it is fast. It does not scrape the pipe wall clean, so grease and scale can narrow the line again within weeks.

That is why we explain what the cable brought back. Hair and soap mean the fix is done. Roots, a gritty sludge or pieces of pipe mean the line needs a camera look and probably sewer line repair. We would rather tell you that on the first visit than clear the same drain three times.

Tubs, showers and drains that leak

Shower and tub drains clog with hair and soap, and most clear in one visit. A drain that clears and then leaves a damp spot, a stained ceiling below, or a musty smell may have a leaking drain line, not just a clog. In that case we stop clearing and use leak detection to find where the water is escaping.

What affects the price of drain cleaning

Which drain it is

A fixture trap is a smaller job than a main house drain, and a cleanout makes the main line much easier to reach.

How far the clog sits

A blockage ten feet in is quick. One a hundred feet down needs more cable and more time.

The cause

Hair and paper clear fast. Roots, grease buildup and broken pipe call for jetting or repair on top of cabling.

Camera inspection

A camera adds time but shows the actual pipe condition, which decides whether you need cleaning or repair.

Time of day

After-hours and weekend dispatch is available at any hour. Ask for the clear price before work starts.

A note on insurance

Homeowners insurance usually covers sudden water damage, such as a burst pipe, but a slow clog and the cleaning of it are typically a maintenance cost. Check your policy if a backup has already damaged floors or drywall.

When cleaning is not enough

Some drains need more than a cable. If the camera shows a cracked pipe, a sag that holds water, or roots pushing through a joint, the fix moves from cleaning to repair. We show you the video, explain the options and let you decide.

Grease is the classic example. A busy kitchen fights it daily, and a family home sees it once or twice a year. For shops, offices and rentals, our commercial plumbing team handles after-hours drain calls too.

If the stoppage looks like a full main line, sewer line cleaning clears the longer run to the street, and a sewer camera inspection shows the pipe from end to end.
