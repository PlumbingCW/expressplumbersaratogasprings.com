How we work

A plumbing call is stressful when water is involved. We keep the process plain: you describe the problem, a plumber looks at it, and you hear the options and the price before anything starts. If the job changes once a wall or pipe is opened, we stop and talk to you first.

What you can count on

  • Licensed plumbers on every job
  • A clear price before work starts
  • Same-day appointments when the schedule allows
  • 24/7 dispatch, including nights, weekends and holidays

What we do not do

Saying no is part of being honest. We do not do structural foundation repair, exterior excavation for waterproofing, or electrical wiring. When a problem needs another trade, we tell you who to look for and why, so you are not paying for a visit that cannot fix it.

How to check any plumber's license

Utah's Division of Occupational and Professional Licensing runs a public Verify a License lookup. Search a name or business there before you hire anyone, including us. It takes a minute, and it is the best protection a homeowner has.

If you are calling for someone else, give the dispatcher the address and a contact who can let the plumber in.

We serve Saratoga Springs, Lehi, Eagle Mountain and American Fork. If you are somewhere nearby, call and ask. We would rather tell you the truth about whether we can reach you than keep you waiting.

What a typical visit looks like

The plumber arrives, listens to what you have seen and heard, and looks at the fixture, pipe or appliance before touching anything. Then they explain what they found in plain words and give you the options with a price for each. You decide, and work starts after you agree.

When the repair is done, the plumber tests it. For a drain, that means running water until it flows clean. For a water heater, it means checking the connections, the relief valve and the temperature. For a leak, it means confirming the pipe holds and the meter has stopped.

For a toilet or faucet, it means running the fixture several times and checking every joint for moisture. Before leaving, the plumber tells you what was done and what to watch for.

If the problem is bigger than the first look suggested, we stop and explain before going further. That rule matters most on emergency calls, when a homeowner is tired, stressed and standing in water.

Licensing and insurance in plain terms

A plumbing license means the state has verified that a person met training and testing requirements. Insurance protects your property if something goes wrong on the job. You should expect both from anyone who works inside your walls, and you should be able to check them.

Our plumbers are licensed, and the company is licensed and insured. If you hire anyone else for plumbing work, ask for the license class and look it up before the job starts. Utah's licensing division lists plumbing license classes, such as journeyman and residential plumber, on its public lookup, so you can see what a given license allows.
