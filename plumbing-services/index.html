What every service has in common

The same rules apply to all of it. Our plumbers are licensed, the company is licensed and insured, and you get a clear price before work starts. Same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows, and the dispatch line answers 24/7.

Local conditions shape the work. Most homes in Saratoga Springs were built after the city incorporated in 2001, which means newer plastic drain lines and newer water heaters than you would find in an old downtown. The city also runs a separate pressurized irrigation system, so a leak in the yard is not always a leak in the house.

How to choose a plumbing service

Start with the symptom, not the pipe. Water on the floor points to the leak and pipe pages. A slow or backed-up drain points to the drain and sewer pages. No hot water, or a tank that is leaking, points to the water heater pages.

If you are comparing a repair with a replacement, the service pages explain how we decide. A water heater that leaks from the tank is usually done, while one that only runs lukewarm may need a part. A pipe with one pinhole leak may need a patch, while a house with repeated pinholes may need a repipe. We would rather explain the difference than sell you the bigger job.

Every service page ends with a short list of common questions, so you can see what to expect before you call.

Planned work such as a new water heater, a repipe or a water softener is also listed here. Those jobs are scheduled ahead of time, and you get a clear price before work starts. For anything urgent, the 24/7 line is always open.
