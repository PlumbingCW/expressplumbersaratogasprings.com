Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Saratoga Springs, UT

A burst pipe can put hundreds of gallons into a home in an hour. Our plumbers shut off, repair and replace broken pipes in Saratoga Springs at any hour.

Burst pipe repair in Saratoga Springs, UT means stopping water from a split or broken pipe, cutting out the damaged section and replacing it with sound pipe and fittings. If water is pouring from a wall, ceiling or crawlspace right now, our 24/7 emergency line is open and a dispatcher can get a plumber moving.

Do the first three things before you call. Close the main water valve, stay out of any room where water is near outlets or the breaker panel, and open a faucet at the lowest point in the house to relieve pressure. Same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows, and night and weekend calls are answered like any other.

Shut it off first: what to do in the first ten minutes

Speed matters because a broken supply line keeps pushing water until the valve is closed. Every minute adds to the damage. These steps are in order for a reason.

  1. Close the main shutoffIt is usually where the line enters the home, often in a garage, utility room or basement. Turn it clockwise until it stops. A ball valve with a lever closes with a quarter turn.
  2. Drain the linesOpen a faucet at the lowest level, and one on the top floor. Gravity pulls the remaining water out of the broken run.
  3. Cut power to wet areasIf water is near outlets, appliances or the panel, do not stand in it. If you cannot reach the breaker safely, call the line and tell the dispatcher.
  4. Turn off the water heaterWith no incoming water, a heater can overheat. Switch off the electric breaker, or set a gas heater to pilot or off.
  5. Document before you cleanPhotograph and video the pipe, the water and every damaged item. Insurance adjusters want the evidence, and a note on the time you found it helps.

If you cannot find the shutoff or it will not turn, say so on the call. A plumber can often shut the water off at another valve on your side of the line.

When a burst pipe is an emergency

Some pipe problems wait for a morning call. A true burst does not. Water at supply pressure can fill a basement or soak drywall fast, and wet insulation and wiring raise the hazard.

Call the 24/7 line right away if you see

  • Water spraying or flowing from a wall, ceiling, floor or pipe you can see
  • A ceiling that is bulging, dripping or beginning to sag
  • A sudden loss of water pressure with a wet spot appearing
  • The sound of rushing water with every fixture off
  • Water reaching outlets, a breaker panel or a furnace

A slow drip at a joint is a smaller problem, though it should still be fixed soon. Call at night or on a weekend if you are unsure, since describing it to a dispatcher costs nothing. Keep children and pets out of the wet area, and do not use the affected room until it is checked.

Cracked and broken pipe repair

Not every failed pipe bursts with a spray. Cracks and hairline splits leak slowly, leave stains and drive up bills. The pipe may have been bumped, corroded, stressed by a fitting or damaged by a past repair. We find the failure, cut back to solid pipe and join new pipe with the right fittings for the material.

For copper we use soldered or press fittings. For PEX we use crimp or expansion connections. The choice depends on the pipe already in your wall, not our preference. If a section is cracked in several places, we explain when replacing a longer run costs less than patching it again.

Copper pipe repair

Copper handles heat and pressure well, though pinholes, loose joints and a split from freezing still occur. A pinhole can be fixed with a short coupling. A longer pitted area is better replaced. We test the repair under pressure before we close the wall, and we check nearby joints that may share the problem.

Water chemistry can wear copper over time, so if you want to know about your supply, ask your water utility for its annual water quality report and test your tap.

Tracing a leaking pipe that is hidden

Sometimes the first sign is a stain, not a spray. If the pipe is in a wall or under a slab, finding the source comes before any repair. Our leak detection page explains the listening and thermal tools we use, and many burst pipe calls begin there.

Once the leak is pinpointed, we open the smallest area that gives access. That limits drywall repair and costs. Patching drywall and paint afterward is a finish trade's job, not ours, so we leave the opening neat and ready.

Frozen pipes in Utah County winters

Utah County winters can bring freezing weather, and water in an exposed pipe can freeze and split it. The split often hides until the ice melts, so water appears after the thaw. If a pipe is frozen but not yet leaking, keep the main valve within reach.

Thawing a frozen pipe the safe way

  • Open the faucet on the frozen line so water has somewhere to go as ice melts
  • Warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer or warm towels, working from the faucet end back
  • Never use an open flame, a torch or a heat gun on a pipe
  • If you see a split or water appears, close the main valve at once
  • Call us if a frozen pipe is inside a wall, crawlspace or exterior wall you cannot reach

Thawing must be gradual and safe. Fast heat can damage the pipe or cause a fire. After thawing, we check the line for splits, since a pipe can look fine and still leak once pressure returns.

Drain pipe and waste line repair

Not every broken pipe is a pressurized supply line. A cracked drain pipe carries wastewater, so the signs are a sewage smell, a damp floor near a toilet or a stain below a bathroom. Drain breaks inside walls and under slabs often need a camera to confirm.

If the break is inside the house, we repair or replace the section. If it is the line out to the street, our sewer line repair page covers that work, and clogs without a break belong to drain cleaning.

Pipe replacement or patch: how we decide

A patch is right when the pipe around the break is sound. A replacement is right when the pipe is corroded, brittle or leaking in more than one place. We show you the failed section so you can see the reason for the recommendation.

If your house keeps developing leaks on the same lines, a larger plan may be cheaper over time. Our repiping page covers whole-home and partial options. After any burst, ask about the water heater connections too, since the same pressure surge can stress them.

What affects the cost of a burst pipe repair

Where the pipe is

A visible pipe in a utility room is faster to repair than one in a finished wall, ceiling or slab.

Pipe material and length

Copper, PEX and galvanized each use different parts and methods. Longer runs need more material and time.

Water damage and access

Opening drywall and removing wet material is separate work. We tell you what we will and will not do.

How many failures there are

One split is a small repair. Several pinholes may point to a bigger replacement.

After-hours dispatch

A note on insurance

Homeowners insurance often covers sudden burst pipe damage to floors and walls, but may not pay for fixing the pipe itself. Gradual leaks are often excluded, so read your policy and tell your insurer early.
