Saratoga Springs and the nearby neighborhoods

In Saratoga Springs we serve homes and businesses across the city, from the Utah Lake shoreline and Pelican Point to Harvest Hills, Pelican Bay and The Benches. Calls from any of them are answered by the same 24/7 dispatch.

Not sure whether your address is covered? Call (385) 488-1892 and give the street and ZIP code. A dispatcher will tell you right away. If you are calling about a rental or a second home, give the address of the property and the name of someone who can let the plumber in.

How coverage works

Coverage means a licensed plumber can be dispatched to your address. It does not mean a fixed arrival time, because the schedule, traffic and the number of open emergencies decide the real timing. When you call, the dispatcher checks the schedule and tells you what to expect for your address.

Tell the dispatcher your street address and ZIP code at the start of the call. Several of our towns share a ZIP code with a neighboring community, and the street address settles which service area you are in.

Four towns, four water systems

Plumbing here depends on where the water comes from. Saratoga Springs culinary water is groundwater plus surface water from the Central Utah Water Conservancy District. Eagle Mountain runs its own water department with wells and the same district supply. Lehi draws on wells, springs and district water, while American Fork relies on wells and springs.

None of the city water reports we checked list a hardness value, so we do not guess at one. If scale on fixtures or a water heater is a worry, ask your water utility and test your tap. Our water softener and filtration page explains how.

Neighborhoods and landmarks we know

In Saratoga Springs, calls come from Harvest Hills, Pelican Bay, The Benches and the streets near Redwood Road and Pioneer Crossing, as well as from homes near the Utah Lake shoreline, Pelican Point and the marina. Knowing the area helps the dispatcher place your address quickly.

Newer homes dominate this part of Utah County, which shapes the calls. Water heaters, supply lines and hose bibs are common repairs, while whole-house repiping is more often a question in older parts of Lehi and American Fork.
