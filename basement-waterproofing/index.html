Basement Waterproofing in Saratoga Springs, UT

Before you pay for waterproofing, find out whether the water is from a pipe or from the ground. Our plumbers handle the plumbing side of keeping basements dry.

Updated 2026-10-15 · Written by the Saratoga Springs Express Plumbing team

Call (385) 488-1892

Basement waterproofing in Saratoga Springs, UT, from a plumber's side, means controlling water with drains, sumps, discharge lines and fixes to leaks, not repairing foundation walls or excavating around them. We work out whether the water comes from a pipe or from the ground, and we handle the parts that are plumbing. Our 24/7 dispatch line can send a plumber if a leak turns into active flooding.

Waterproofing is a planned job, so the first step is diagnosis. Wet walls, a damp floor or a musty smell can come from a leaking pipe, a poorly sloped yard or groundwater. The right fix depends entirely on which of these it is, so we sort that out first.

Is the water from a pipe or from the ground?

Check these before you spend money on drainage. A plumbing leak is fixed at a pipe. Groundwater is managed with drains and pumps. The test is simple.

Telling plumbing water from groundwater
CluePoints to plumbingPoints to groundwater
Water meterDial moves with every tap offDial is still
TimingConstant, regardless of weatherShows up after rain, snowmelt or irrigation
LocationUnder a sink, near a water heater or a washerAlong the base of walls or at the slab edge
Foil testMoisture forms on the room sideMoisture forms under the foil, against the wall
Water clarityClean, or sewage smell from drainsMuddy or mineral-stained

To run the meter test, turn off all fixtures and watch the dial for 15 minutes. For the foil test, tape a square of foil to the wall for 24 hours.

What plumbing can do about basement water

A plumber handles the parts of water control that involve pipes and pumps. That includes sump pits and pumps, floor drains and their connections, drains from window wells, and fixing any leak that is adding to the problem.

Our sump pump installation and repair page covers pits and pumps in detail. This page is about how the pieces fit together.

What to note before you call

  • Meter dial moves while every fixture is off
  • Water appears only after rain, snowmelt or a sprinkler cycle
  • A damp patch sits under a sink, water heater or washer hookup
  • A floor drain bubbles or smells of sewage
  • A window well holds water against the glass

Interior drains and the sump

Some homes have a perimeter drain inside the footing, which carries water to a sump pit. The pit's pump then pushes it out of the house. If the drain clogs or the pump fails, water rises.

We can camera, clear and reconnect these drains. We do not cut and install a new perimeter drain system as a structural job, since that involves the foundation. Ask us about a new pit or pump.

Floor drains that overflow or smell

A basement floor drain connects to the sewer, and it backs up when the main line is blocked. A dry trap can also let sewer gas into the room. Pouring water in refills it.

If a floor drain bubbles or fills, stop using fixtures and call. That is a sewer problem and not groundwater, and sewer line cleaning clears it.

Window wells and downspout discharge

Many wet basements start outside. A window well that fills with water, or a downspout that drops water beside the foundation, sends it down along the wall.

Extend downspouts well away from the house and slope the soil away. A window well can have a drain at the bottom connected to the footing drain, and we can clear that. Do not tie a drain or pump discharge into the irrigation or drinking water lines.

Spring water: irrigation and snowmelt

Saratoga Springs charges its pressurized irrigation system the week of April 15, so sprinklers that spray near the foundation can add to spring dampness. If water shows up in spring, check where the sprinklers aim before you assume the foundation is leaking.

The system is drained the week of October 15, so a wet basement in winter is less likely to be sprinkler water.

What we do not do, and who to call

We are plumbers. We do not repair foundation cracks, install exterior waterproofing membranes, or excavate around a foundation. If water comes in through a wall crack, a floor-wall joint or a bowed wall, call a foundation specialist.

Also call a specialist if the problem persists after the plumbing checks come back clean. Bring your findings from the meter and foil tests. They help.
