Sewer Camera Inspection in Saratoga Springs, UT

A camera shows what a cable cannot: cracks, roots, bellies and breaks. Our plumbers inspect and locate sewer lines in Saratoga Springs, UT.

Updated 2026-10-15 · Written by the Saratoga Springs Express Plumbing team

Call (385) 488-1892

Sewer camera inspection in Saratoga Springs, UT is a look inside your sewer line using a waterproof camera on a flexible rod, which shows the pipe's condition, its path and the location of any problem. It turns a guess about a clog or a smell into something you can see on a screen. We run the camera through a cleanout or a pulled toilet and note what it finds.

This is a planned visit, though our 24/7 dispatch line takes inspection calls at any hour if a backup demands it. Many people book one before buying a house, after repeated clogs or before any digging, because the video decides what work, if any, the line really needs.

What the camera actually shows

The camera head has a light and a lens, and it sends live video to a monitor. As it moves down the line, we watch for the pipe material, the joints and anything abnormal.

Cracks, roots and offsets show up clearly. So do grease coatings, scale, bellies where water pools, and breaks. Some cameras also carry a transmitter, which lets a locator on the surface mark the depth and path of the pipe.

What shows up on the monitor

  • Cracks, missing sections and collapsed pipe
  • Roots entering at a joint, and how thick they are
  • Bellies or sags that hold water and waste
  • Offset joints, where one pipe has shifted away from the next
  • Grease, scale or buildup narrowing the pipe

Locating the line before anyone digs

The camera is only half of a locate. With a transmitter in the camera head, a surface receiver tells us where the pipe runs and how deep it sits. We can mark the path with paint or flags.

That matters because the pipe rarely runs where people think. Before digging, call 811 at least three business days ahead. A utility will not mark a line it does not own, so a private line may need our locate to avoid a dig in the wrong place.

Buying a house: the pre-purchase inspection

A sewer line cannot be seen during a standard walkthrough, and repairs are expensive when they appear after closing. A camera pass shows the line's condition before you commit.

Most homes in Saratoga Springs were built after 2001, so many lines are plastic and in decent shape. A camera confirms it and finds the exceptions, like root intrusion or a settled section. Ask the seller for cleanout access, and tell us your deadline when you call.

What you get after the inspection

We explain what we saw as we see it, and note the findings in writing. For a buyer, that can include a recorded video and a short summary of the problems, with their approximate location on the line.

Written notes are useful for a seller negotiation, a landlord, an insurer or a later repair. Ask for the notes when you book so we plan the visit around them.

Can you actually see the problem?

A camera is only as good as its view. Dirty water, a full pipe or a long run limits what it can show, and a line with heavy buildup has to be cleaned first. We say so when that happens.

Some problems do not show. A pinhole or a joint that leaks only under load may escape a camera, and then we use other methods. For a hidden leak that the camera cannot confirm, our leak detection work uses listening and pressure testing.

Where a camera inspection leads

The camera is a first step. If it shows clean pipe, you are done. If it shows buildup, sewer line cleaning or hydro jetting comes next. If it shows damage, sewer line repair covers the options. We inspect lines across Saratoga Springs and in Lehi too.
