Should I Repipe My House? Saratoga Springs, UT Homes

Repiping makes sense when leaks repeat, water runs brown from pipes themselves or flow is choked by corroded lines. For many newer Saratoga Springs homes, a repair or partial repipe is enough.

Pipes and leaks · 7 min read · Updated 2026-10-15 · Written by the Saratoga Springs Express Plumbing team

Call (385) 488-1892

You should repipe when your supply lines are failing in more than one place, when the pipe material itself is known to fail, or when corrosion has cut your water flow. One pinhole leak does not call for new pipes. Three leaks in a year, brown water that starts at the pipe, or galvanized steel throughout usually does. The decision rests on the pattern and the material, not on age alone. We explain how below.

Most homes in Saratoga Springs were built after 2001, so a whole-house repipe is less common here than in older towns. That is good news for many owners, and it also means you should rule out simpler causes first. Our decision table below shows how to read the signs.

Pinhole leaks: one is a repair, a pattern is a decision

A pinhole is a tiny hole worn through the wall of a pipe, often from the inside. One can come from a manufacturing flaw or a poor fitting, and a clean repair ends it. Several in a short span suggest the whole run is thinning.

Before you choose, find every leak. Leak detection with acoustic listening, a pressure test or a thermal camera shows where water is escaping and how many separate spots you have. That count is what makes the decision, not a guess.

Discolored water: pipe, water heater or the street?

Rust-colored water is a symptom, not a diagnosis. If it appears only on the hot side, the water heater tank may be shedding sediment or corrosion. If it appears on the cold side at every tap, the supply pipes are suspect.

A quick check helps: run the cold tap nearest the main for a few minutes. If it clears, the sediment is local to one branch. If it stays discolored, test the water at the meter side or ask your utility whether it is flushing mains in your area.

Low pressure that only some fixtures have

Pressure that is weak at every fixture while an outdoor hose bib is strong suggests a restriction inside the house. Galvanized steel is the classic cause because mineral buildup and rust narrow the inside of the pipe. Pressure that is weak at one fixture is usually a clogged aerator or worn cartridge, which a simple repair fixes.

What a plumber checks before recommending a repipe

The inspection that should come first

  • Pipe material at the main shut-off, water heater and under sinks, since the visible pipe usually matches the hidden run
  • Static water pressure at an outdoor spigot, compared with pressure at fixtures inside
  • A pressure test of the supply lines with all fixtures off, to count leaks that are not visible
  • The interior of an old pipe, where a section can be cut out and examined for scale or pitting
  • Whether leaks cluster on one run, which supports a partial repipe

Galvanized steel and other older pipe

Galvanized steel was common in older houses. It rusts from the inside, so the opening narrows and flow drops long before the pipe leaks. If your house is plumbed in it, low pressure and brown water at every tap are expected, and repair usually only delays the repipe.

Homes in this city were mostly built after 2001, so galvanized supply pipe is unlikely here. It is far more likely in older housing in other towns, which is why a repipe is a common question there and a rare one in newer neighborhoods.

What a repipe looks like and what to expect

A repipe replaces the supply lines from the main shut-off to each fixture. Plumbers usually open small access holes in the drywall, run new lines and patch the wall. The water is off for most of a work day or more, depending on the house.

A partial repipe, such as one bathroom or one leaking run, costs less and may be all you need. Our repiping page explains scope, and we can tell you honestly whether a partial job covers the problem.

What changes the cost of a repipe

Size of the house

More fixtures and longer runs mean more pipe and more labor.

Access to the framing

Open ceilings in a basement make the work simpler. Finished walls need more patching.

Material choice

PEX and copper differ in material cost and in install time.

Whole house or partial

One bathroom or a single leaking run costs far less than every fixture.

Drywall repair

Access holes must be patched and painted, and that can be handled separately.

Nearby towns with older housing stock

Older homes are more likely to have old supply piping. Lehi has a historic core settled in 1850 alongside very recent growth, so house age there varies a lot. We serve Lehi and American Fork and see different pipe materials depending on when a house was built.

Saratoga Springs: what the city water report says and does not say

The city's 2024 Annual Drinking Water Quality Report lists total dissolved solids of 144 to 672 ppm against a limit of 2000 ppm. It lists lead at 1.7 ppb and copper at 0.076 ppm at the 90th percentile, both below action levels. The city and Central Utah Water Conservancy District chlorinate the water.

The report lists no hardness value. If you see scale or pinholes, ask your utility for the annual report and test your tap before blaming the pipes. Those results are for sampled homes, not for yours.
