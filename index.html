When to call the emergency line right now

Some plumbing problems can wait for a weekday morning. The ones below cannot, because water keeps moving until someone stops it.

Call (385) 488-1892 for any of these

  • A burst, frozen or cracked pipe, or water spraying from a fitting
  • Sewage or gray water coming up through a floor drain, tub or shower
  • A water heater that is leaking, flooding a closet or has no hot water at all
  • A toilet that is overflowing and will not stop
  • A water meter that keeps spinning when every fixture in the house is off
  • Water on a ceiling or wall that is spreading

While you wait, shut off the main water valve if water is flowing and you can reach it safely. Keep people and pets away from standing water near outlets. If you smell gas, leave the building first and call the gas utility or 911 from outside.

Plumbing problems that follow the Utah County calendar

Plumbing here moves with the seasons. Freezing weather can crack exposed lines and hose bibs, and thawing too fast can make a hidden crack worse. Spring brings the pressurized irrigation system back to life, and the city charges it the week of April 15, which is when sprinkler line leaks show up.

In fall the city drains pressurized irrigation the week of October 15. Leave your individual shutoff valves open so the system can drain, and close the ball valve after November 1. Irrigation water is not for drinking, and its lines must never connect to the drinking water plumbing.

Year-round, water heaters are the other big source of calls. A tank that is leaking from the bottom is usually finished, while a unit that is only lukewarm may need a simple repair. Our water heater repair and water heater replacement pages explain how to tell the difference.
