How to use these guides

Each guide starts with the short answer, then explains why. Where a topic depends on your property, such as a slab leak or a sewer line, we say so and tell you what a plumber can check that you cannot.

Local facts come from city pages and other sources we could verify, and each guide names its source. Cost ranges that appear in a guide come from named national sources and are labeled that way. They are not quotes from us, so call for a clear price on your own job.

When a guide is not enough

A guide can tell you what is likely going on, but it cannot see inside a wall or a buried pipe. If water is spreading, sewage is backing up or a water heater is leaking, skip the reading and call (385) 488-1892. The line is staffed 24/7.

For anything planned, such as a water heater swap or a repipe, a guide helps you ask better questions. Write down what you learn and bring it to the call, so the plumber can give you a clear price that fits your home.
