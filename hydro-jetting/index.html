Emergency Hydro Jetting in Saratoga Springs, UT

When a cable only pokes a hole in the clog, a hydro-jet scours the pipe wall clean. Our plumbers jet drain and sewer lines in Saratoga Springs at any hour.

Updated 2026-10-15 · Written by the Saratoga Springs Express Plumbing team

Call (385) 488-1892

Hydro jetting in Saratoga Springs, UT is a pump and hose that send high-pressure water through a nozzle to scour a drain or sewer line. A cable punches a hole through a clog, but the jet washes the pipe wall, pushing grease, sludge, scale and fine roots toward the city sewer. We use it when a cable stopped short, a line clogs every few weeks or a camera shows heavy buildup. Our 24/7 emergency line is answered at any hour.

If the line is backing up right now, stop running water, keep the washer and dishwasher off, and keep children and pets away from standing water. Do not pour another bottle of chemical opener into a full pipe. Nights and weekends are dispatched the same way as weekday calls, and same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows.

The cable went in and the clog came back anyway

This is the most common reason people call us about jetting. A crew or a rental machine cleared the drain, it ran fine for a few weeks, and then the gurgling started again. Sometimes the cable stopped short and could not get past a certain point.

Both stories usually point to the same thing. The blockage is not one plug of paper. It is a coating on the pipe wall that keeps growing, and the cable only drills a small hole through the middle of it.

Signs a cable alone is not finishing the job

  • The cable stopped at the same distance every time and would not go further
  • The drain cleared, then slowed again within weeks
  • The cable came back wrapped in grease, gray sludge or fine hair-like roots
  • A kitchen line that smells sour even after it drains
  • Gurgling in a tub or floor drain when the washer runs, even after a service visit

We often cable first to open a path, then jet to clean. The camera decides the order.

What a jet does inside the pipe

The nozzle has several small ports. Most spray backward, which pulls the hose forward through the line, and some spray forward or sideways to cut through the clog. Water pressure and flow do the work, so there is no blade scraping the pipe.

  1. Camera lookWe run a drain camera first when the line allows it. That shows the pipe material, the joints and any cracks before water pressure goes in.
  2. Open the lineAt a cleanout or a pulled toilet, we feed the hose in with a nozzle sized to the pipe.
  3. Work forward and backWe jet toward the clog, then pull the hose back slowly while the nozzle scours the walls.
  4. Check the resultA second camera pass shows a clean wall, or shows the damage that was hiding behind the buildup.

Is jetting safe for your pipes?

On sound plastic pipe, jetting is routine. Most homes in Saratoga Springs were built after the city incorporated in 2001, so plastic drain lines with smooth joints are the common case. Even so, we do not assume.

Old, cracked, collapsed or badly offset pipe is a different story. Water pressure can widen a crack or wash soil in through a gap. If the camera shows a broken line, we stop and quote the repair. If the pipe is an older material, tell us when you call, and we will use a lower pressure setting or recommend cabling and a camera first.

Our rule for damaged pipe

Camera first. If we see a crack, a missing section or a bad joint, we do not jet through it. We show you the video and explain the options on sewer line repair before any more pressure goes in.

Roots and grease: what jetting removes and what it does not

Grease and sludge are the best jobs for a jet because the water reaches every part of the pipe wall. Fine root hairs wash out too, and a cutter nozzle can trim heavier roots at a joint.

Roots come back unless the joint that lets them in is sealed. If the camera shows roots entering through a cracked joint, the jet clears them for now and a repair keeps them out. We tell you honestly which of the two you are paying for.

If the clog hides on a stubborn run to the street, sewer line cleaning covers the main line from the cleanout, and a sewer camera inspection records what the jet found.

When a jetting job is an emergency

A line that backs up into a tub, shower or floor drain is an emergency, and so is one where sewage is reaching the floor. Water in a lower level has nowhere else to go, and it can spread under flooring within minutes.

Call the 24/7 line, shut off the water you control, and move rugs and boxes away from the drain. If a crew already cabled the line today and it is backing up again, say so. That tells us to bring the jet and skip the second cable pass. After-hours calls get the same equipment as daytime ones.

What to do while you wait

  • Stop running water in sinks, tubs and the washer
  • Keep children and pets out of the standing water
  • Do not pour chemical opener into a line that is already full
  • Tell the dispatcher if a crew already cabled the line

What affects the price of hydro jetting

Pipe size and length

A four-inch main with a long run to the street takes more hose and more time than a short kitchen branch.

What is in the pipe

Light grease washes out quickly. Heavy scale or thick roots call for several passes and a cutter nozzle.

Access

A cleanout makes the work straightforward. Pulling a toilet or working from a roof vent costs more time.

Camera work

A camera pass before and after shows the real condition and adds to the visit.

Time of day

Nights and weekends are available. Ask for the clear price before work starts.

A note on insurance

Homeowners insurance usually covers sudden water damage, but routine jetting to prevent clogs is typically a maintenance cost. If a backup has already ruined flooring, check your policy and keep the written findings.
