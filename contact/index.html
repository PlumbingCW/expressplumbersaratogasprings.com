What happens when you call

A dispatcher answers, asks for your address and ZIP code, and asks what is happening. They will tell you what to do while you wait, such as where to find the main water shutoff, and then book a licensed plumber.

You get a clear price before work starts. If the problem turns out to be bigger than it looked, the plumber stops and explains the options before going further.

Before you call

Helpful details to have ready

  • Your address and ZIP code
  • What you see, hear or smell, and where in the house
  • Whether water is still flowing and whether you found the main shutoff
  • Whether anyone in the building is in danger

If you smell gas, leave the building first and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. Do not use switches or phones inside.

Planned work and questions

For a planned job such as a water heater swap or a repipe, call during the day and describe what you have in mind. The plumber will explain the steps and the price before anything is scheduled. Questions about the website or this policy can go to the email address above.
