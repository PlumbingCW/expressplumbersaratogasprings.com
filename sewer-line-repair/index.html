Emergency Sewer Line Repair in Saratoga Springs, UT

A sewer backup, a wet patch in the yard or roots in the pipe all call for a clear diagnosis first. Our plumbers repair sewer lines in Saratoga Springs day or night.

Sewer line repair in Saratoga Springs, UT means fixing the pipe that carries wastewater from your house to the city sewer, whether that is a cracked section, a root-damaged joint, a sag that holds water or a pipe that has collapsed. We find the break with a drain camera and then choose between a spot repair, a pipe liner or a replacement. Our emergency line is open 24/7 for backups and starts every repair with a camera.

If sewage is coming up in the house, read the safety steps below before you do anything else. Stay out of the water, do not run any fixtures, and call. Nights, weekends and same-day calls are dispatched when the schedule allows, and the first step is always a camera so we repair only what is broken.

Signs the sewer line is damaged, not just clogged

A single clog clears and stays clear. A damaged line keeps coming back, because the pipe itself is the problem. The pattern is more useful than any one symptom.

A damaged line often shows these patterns

  • Repeat backups in the lowest drain, even after cable cleaning
  • A sewer smell in the yard, basement or garage that comes and goes
  • A soft, green or sunken patch of lawn along the path of the line
  • Several fixtures gurgling at once when one flushes
  • Roots on the cable or camera when a line is cleaned

Sewer backup: what to do in the first ten minutes

A sewage backup is an emergency because sewage carries bacteria and can spread across a floor in minutes. Treat the water as contaminated, whatever color it looks.

  1. Stop the waterDo not flush, run the sink, start the washer or use the dishwasher. Every gallon you add goes to the same blocked line.
  2. Stay out of itKeep children, pets and yourself out of standing sewage. Wear boots and gloves if you must cross the room.
  3. Cut power to wet areasIf water reaches outlets or appliances, turn off those breakers only if the panel is dry and you can reach it without stepping in water.
  4. Call usTell the dispatcher which drains are backing up and where water is reaching the floor. We camera the line on arrival.
  5. Document itTake photos before you move anything. Your insurer may ask for them.

Do not try to wash the floor with a hose before the line is clear. You push sewage into cracks and make the cleanup worse.

Spot repair for a cracked line with root intrusion

Roots do not break a healthy pipe. They find a crack or a loose joint, enter for the moisture, and then thicken until the opening grows. If the camera shows roots entering at one joint and the rest of the line is sound, a spot repair is usually the cleanest answer.

We locate the break from above with a pipe locator, dig a short trench, cut out the bad section and fit new pipe with proper fall. Before any digging, 811 must be called at least three business days ahead, and a utility will not mark a line it does not own, so we locate your private line ourselves.

Trenchless pipe lining

Lining sends a resin-soaked tube into the old pipe, then cures it in place to form a new smooth pipe inside the old one. It works when the pipe is cracked, leaking at joints or invaded by roots but still round. It can save a driveway, a patio or a mature tree.

Lining reduces the pipe diameter slightly, and it does not fix a pipe that has dropped or collapsed. The camera tells us whether the pipe qualifies. If it does not, we say so and quote a replacement instead.

Cast iron pipe and the age of your home

Most homes in Saratoga Springs went up after 2001, so plastic sewer pipe is the common case here. Cast iron is rare and mostly shows up in older homes or additions with older plumbing.

If your home does have cast iron, it can corrode from the inside and scale up, and that scale catches paper and roots. We repair cast iron by cutting out the bad section and joining plastic with the right couplings, or by lining when the pipe is still sound. Tell us the pipe material if you know it.

Drain pipe repair inside the house

A leaking drain pipe under a sink, behind a wall or under a floor is repaired in the same way, with a section cut out and replaced. We pair it with leak detection when the wet spot is hard to trace.

If you have a septic tank

Saratoga Springs is served by the city sanitary sewer system, and Timpanogos Special Service District treats the sewage. Most homes in the city connect to it, so we do not offer septic tank service.

If your property is on a septic tank or outside city sewer, call (385) 488-1892 and describe the layout. We will tell you honestly whether the pipe work between the house and the tank is something we can handle.
