Brown water, weak pressure or a soggy yard: sorting out the water line

The first question we ask is where the problem shows. Discolored water at every tap points to the supply pipe or the water heater. Weak pressure at one faucet points to that fixture, while weak pressure everywhere points to a restricted valve or a leak on the line from the meter.

Older homes in the core may still carry older supply pipe, and pipe that has corroded inside restricts flow long before it leaks. We confirm by looking at the material, checking pressure and cutting a short section where needed. Our repiping page explains when a partial or whole-house repipe is the honest answer and when a spot repair is enough.

A soggy patch near the street is a different problem. If the meter spins with every fixture off, the leak is on the buried line, and water line repair is the next step after we locate it. Lehi's Main Street Historic District is on the National Register, so tell us about any exterior or street work before a trench is dug.

Gurgling drains and sewage smell in a town with an 1850s grid

In the oldest blocks, a sewer line that has been in the ground for decades may have picked up roots, offsets or bellies. A drain that clears, then clogs again within weeks, is the sign to look at the pipe instead of cabling it again. We run a camera before we recommend anything.

If the video shows a cracked or collapsed section, sewer line repair is the answer, and we show you the footage first. If it only shows grease or paper, a cable or hydro-jet clears it and you are done. Anything backing up into a basement floor drain is an emergency, so call the 24/7 line right away.

Water heaters in Lehi: tanks, sediment and water quality

Lehi's 2024 water report lists six wells, two springs and surface water from the Central Utah Water Conservancy District, with TDS from 104 to 340 ppm. The report does not list hardness, so test your tap or ask the city for its annual report. Scale and sediment still settle in the bottom of a tank over time, and a tank that cannot heat the whole house by morning is telling you something.

Our water heater replacement team works with failed tanks, and many repairs on thermostats and elements can be done the same day when the schedule allows. If water pools around a tank, shut off the water supply to it and the gas or power, and call the 24/7 line.

Pressurized irrigation goes off on October 15, and it is not drinking water

Lehi tells owners to turn off their pressurized irrigation connection by October 15. The city also states that this water is not treated and is not safe to drink, and the system keeps running year-round for fire hydrants. Never connect it to indoor plumbing or fill drinking bottles from it.

For plumbing calls, the practical point is simple. An outdoor spigot on the irrigation side is not the same as a house hose bib on the culinary side. If you are not sure which one a pipe belongs to, stop using it for anything but sprinklers and tell the dispatcher when you call.

Before we arrive: a Lehi shutoff routine

Before we arrive

Shut off the main

Older homes often have a main valve in a basement or crawl space, and some are stiff from age. Turn it slowly and do not force it. If it will not close, tell us.

Cut power near water

Switch off breakers for any wet room or basement. If the panel is in the wet area, stay out and call us first.

Clear the floor

Move furniture, boxes and stored items off a wet basement floor. A shop vacuum can help only if the outlet is dry and away from standing water.
